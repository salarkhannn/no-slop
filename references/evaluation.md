# Blind evaluation

Use when changing this skill or claiming it generalizes. Evaluate without revealing the
desired visual answer to the implementer.

`evals/cases.json` contains scenarios spanning marketing, dense product UI, media,
enterprise systems, bare codebases, tenant theming, analytics, brand-led consumer
products, and monochrome tools.

For each case, verify the output includes:

- a host-style preserve/question/reject decision;
- a product- and task-specific visual-direction brief with a governing idea, desired audience
  state, evidence carrier, content burden, and visual vocabulary;
- reference analysis that transfers observed traits by responsibility instead of treating a
  reference as a template or answer;
- a preserve/repair/create color decision with named color owners and constraints;
- a rendered achromatic, warm-neutral, and cool-neutral comparison when the neutral
  system is created or materially changed;
- independently tunable accent, action, selection, focus, semantic-state, user-content,
  and chart roles where applicable;
- role-only semantic tokens, with numeric steps confined to primitives;
- light, dark, high-contrast, media, and data checks required by the scenario;
- a rendered preserve/revise/replace typeface decision and an intentional weight hierarchy;
- three structurally different concepts;
- a justified topology and compound interaction model;
- an explicit visual-media decision for each material graphic: product evidence, data
  visualization, explanatory diagram, illustration, or no graphic;
- a semantic skeleton before styling any data visualization or explanatory diagram;
- component and variant records;
- route differentiation where multiple routes exist;
- symmetry contracts for peers;
- credible content, content economy, and state handling;
- rendered narrow, medium, and wide evidence;
- no unexamined ordinary-slop or anti-slop accumulation;
- no decorative chart or diagram whose marks, labels, or relationships fail a retrieval,
  substitution, integrity, redundancy, identity, or scale test;
- no nested visual boundary whose role duplicates its parent;
- no self-awarded score presented as product proof.

Examples are decision traces, not templates. Check whether the implementer transferred the
reasoning—host mode, color ownership, candidate comparison, and role separation—without
copying an example's palette.

Compare failures across cases. If quiet tools and expressive brand worlds converge on the
same density, typeface, weight distribution, typography, base neutral, accent hue, surface
treatment, layout topology, visual medium, or marketing sequence, the skill has overfit even
when each screen is individually competent.
